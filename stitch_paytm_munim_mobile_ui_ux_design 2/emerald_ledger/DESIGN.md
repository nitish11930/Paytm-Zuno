## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an intelligent, reassuring, and ultra-reliable financial companion for Indian retail merchants and small shopkeepers (*dukandaars*). Moving beyond cold transactional utilities, the visual atmosphere balances institutional financial stability with conversational AI ease.

The style unites **Modern Fintech Elegance** with **Tactile Softness**:
- **Dignified & Prosperous:** Deep emerald and dark forest tones communicate prosperity, long-term wealth, and Paytm-grade infrastructure security.
- **Approachable & High-Clarity:** Crisp mint washes, off-white tactile card containers, and luminous mint highlights simplify complex bookkeeping, reconciliations, and voice-assisted audits.
- **Friction-Free Ergonomics:** Generous touch targets (48–56px heights), pill actions, large numerical figures with native Indian Rupee (`₹`) glyph legibility, and high-visibility status tags crafted for hectic retail countertop workflows.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-contrast herbal and forest greens against warm paper surfaces to maintain clear sunlight readability in busy storefront environments.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Green (`#00856A`):** The primary brand anchor for key navigation items, prominent CTAs, active segmented toggles, and positive balances.
- **Deep Emerald (`#003F35`):** Reserved for hero statement surfaces, balance overview headers, and dark modal sheets.
- **Dark Forest (`#002E28`):** Deepest structural base for high-contrast hero modules and dark-mode splash headers.
- **Bright Accent (`#17B880`):** Vibrant mint-green highlight used for AI notification dots, instant verification badges, pulse rings, and confirmation icons.
- **Mint Surface (`#EAF7F2`):** Gentle tinted backdrop for selected quick-filter pills, icon containers, and secondary action wrappers.
- **App Background (`#F7FAF9`):** Soft, non-glare off-white canvas for long inventory checks and ledger scans.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level elevation cards and actionable ledger row containers.
- **Main Text (`#142523`):** High-legibility deep slate for primary labels, ledger figures, and headlines.
- **Secondary Text (`#647572`):** Calm muted sage for subtitles, transaction timestamps, and metadata.
- **Border Subtle (`#E3ECE8`):** 1px structural boundaries dividing transaction rows and search fields.

### Status Tones
- **Merchant Confirmed / Success:** `#00856A` text on `#EAF7F2` container.
- **Payment Matched / Active:** `#17B880` text on `#E5FAF3` container.
- **Needs Review / Warning:** `#B76A13` text on `#FEF6EB` container.
- **Payment Failed / Danger:** `#C64040` text on `#FCEAEA` container.

## Typography

**Plus Jakarta Sans** is selected across all roles for its balanced geometric clarity, humanist open counters, and high distinction between numerals—essential for zero-error financial figures and Indian Rupee notation (`₹`).

### Typography Rules
- **Numerical Hierarchy:** All financial amounts must prioritize weight (`700`) over size where vertical canvas space is limited. Always place the `₹` symbol before figures with a non-breaking space (e.g., `₹ 24,560.00`).
- **Devanagari & Multilingual Alignment:** Plus Jakarta Sans pairs naturally with system Devanagari fonts (such as Noto Sans Devanagari) to maintain consistent baseline heights and vertical alignment when switching languages.
- **Tabular Figures:** Transaction lists and ledger tallies must activate OpenType tabular numbers (`tnum`) to keep decimal points cleanly aligned.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout adheres to an **8pt rhythmic grid** optimized for mobile viewports (baseline width 390px), expanding gracefully into tablet POS cash registers.

### Spatial Discipline
- **Canvas Margins:** Fixed `16px` (`margin-mobile`) horizontal screen padding keeps density intact while preventing edge clipping on curved display glass.
- **Card-Level Spacing:** Interior padding inside primary ledger cards is fixed to `16px` (`space-md`) or `20px` for hero balances.
- **Action Grids:** Quick service icon grids utilize a 4-column fluid container with uniform `8px` gutters (`space-xs`) to support fast one-thumb tapping.
- **Vertical Hierarchy:** Section headers maintain `24px` (`space-lg`) top spacing and `12px` (`space-sm`) bottom gap prior to child lists.

## Elevation & Depth

This system utilizes **Tonal Layering** combined with **Ambient Botanical Shadows** to deliver deep tactile separation without noisy borders.

### Surface Tiers
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#F7FAF9`. Ground-level app viewport.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#FFFFFF` bordered with an imperceptible `1px solid #E3ECE8`. Ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 16px rgba(0, 63, 53, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Modals & Popovers):** `#FFFFFF` paired with an elevated ambient drop shadow: `0 8px 28px rgba(0, 63, 53, 0.08)`.
- **Hero Statement Container:** Solid `#003F35` or radial shift into `#002E28`. Uses internal diffuse lighting (`inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`) to echo hardware POS displays.

### Floating Floating Actions & Bottom Navigation
The bottom bar floats above canvas with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, a `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)` tinted surface, and an upward shadow: `0 -4px 20px rgba(0, 46, 40, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature uses **Organic Curved Rectangles** and **Complete Pill Contours**. 

- **Primary Interactive Elements (Buttons & Status Pills):** Full pill (`9999px`), ensuring tactile comfort and clear affordance for primary mobile inputs.
- **Feature Cards & Financial Summaries:** `rounded-lg` (`16px` to `20px`) providing high boundary definition while remaining friendly and modern.
- **Hero Header Cards:** `24px` radius for major focal surfaces (e.g., wallet balance, AI insight cards).
- **Service Shortcuts & Floating Icon Docks:** `16px` rounded squircles (`rounded-lg`) offering consistent button proportions.
